# Spell explanations and sources

Spell names and sourcebooks in `class-progression.mjs` were gathered from the 2024 D&D Wikidot spell and class tables, then checked against the official 2024 Free Rules where available. `classSpellDetails` stores spell level, school, concentration, ritual status, sourcebook, and an individual reference link. The class-view book filters use the same source names as the campaign toggles. Twelve 2026 D&D Beyond Drops options have a separate DM-review toggle because they are outside the four catalog campaign books.

`spell-glossary.mjs` contains 169 short original effect summaries checked against the linked official 2024 D&D Beyond spell pages. Existing `spellNotes` adds concise notes for some subclass and expansion spells. Remaining entries still show their spell level, school, casting flags, sourcebook, and a direct rules link; the app does not invent an effect description when no checked summary is available.

These summaries omit casting time, components, range, durations, scaling, and full conditions. Follow the linked spell rules for those details. The verified effect summaries are planning aids, not replacement rule text.
